## How to Use This Guide

1. **Start with Basics** - Ensure you understand core concepts
2. **Progress by Topic** - Move through categories systematically
3. **Practice Answers** - Write or speak your answers aloud
4. **Hands-On Practice** - Try commands and scenarios in a lab environment
5. **Review Regularly** - Revisit questions before interviews

## Interview Tips

- **Explain concepts clearly** - Use analogies when helpful
- **Show practical experience** - Reference real scenarios
- **Know your commands** - Be ready to demonstrate kubectl usage
- **Understand trade-offs** - Discuss pros/cons of different approaches
- **Stay current** - Know the latest Kubernetes features (2026)

## Most Common Questions

1. What is Kubernetes and why use it?
2. Explain the difference between a Deployment and a StatefulSet
3. What is a Pod and how does it differ from a container?
4. How does Kubernetes handle networking?
5. What is RBAC and why is it important?
6. Explain the difference between ConfigMap and Secret
7. How do you troubleshoot a Pod that won't start?
8. What is a Service and what types are available?
9. How does horizontal pod autoscaling work?
10. What is etcd and why is it critical?
